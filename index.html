✨ What is Hermes Agent Desktop?

Hermes Agent Desktop is a smart, AI-powered desktop application that brings the power of advanced artificial intelligence right to your laptop or desktop computer. Developed with Nous Research's technology, this app helps you automate tasks, generate content, answer questions, and manage your digital life—all from one easy-to-use interface.

Think of it as your personal AI team member that sits on your desktop, ready to help whenever you need it. Whether you want to draft emails, analyze data, create schedules, or just chat about ideas, Hermes Agent Desktop makes it possible with just a few clicks.

Best of all? It works completely offline and free if you choose to run it with local Ollama models. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no internet required for the core experience.
